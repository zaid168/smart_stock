# SmartStock 📦
### AI-Powered Inventory Management & Blockchain-Based Supply Chain Tracking

## 📌 Overview

SmartStock is an intelligent inventory management system designed for manufacturing industries. It integrates AI-based demand forecasting, QR/Barcode inventory tracking, and blockchain technology to improve inventory management and provide transparent shipment tracking.

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Inventory Management:** Manage raw materials and finished goods.
- **QR/Barcode Tracking:** Simplify product identification and stock updates.
- **AI Demand Forecasting:** Predict future product demand using historical data.
- **Smart Reorder Recommendations:** Identify potential stock shortages and suggest replenishment.
- **Blockchain Shipment Tracking:** Maintain tamper-resistant shipment records.
- **Delivery Verification:** Track goods from factory dispatch to customer receipt.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Backend:** Python FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **ORM:** SQLAlchemy
- **AI/ML:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn
- **Blockchain:** Ethereum, Solidity
- **Smart Contracts:** Hardhat
- **Blockchain Integration:** Ethers.js
- **Containerization:** Docker

## ⚙️ System Workflow

1. Register raw materials and finished products.
2. Manage inventory through manual entry or QR/Barcode scanning.
3. Analyse historical data to forecast future demand.
4. Generate intelligent reorder recommendations.
5. Create shipments when products are dispatched.
6. Record shipment milestones on the blockchain.
7. Verify delivery when customers receive their products.

## 🔗 Blockchain Integration

Smart contracts record important shipment events, including:

- Shipment registration.
- Factory dispatch.
- Transit updates.
- Delivery confirmation.

Detailed application data is stored in PostgreSQL, while important shipment events are recorded on the blockchain to provide a tamper-resistant tracking history.
